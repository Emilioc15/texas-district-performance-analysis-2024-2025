# Texas School District Performance Analysis: 2024–2025 Academic Year

## Project Overview

This project analyzes district-level education data from Texas school districts during the 2024–2025 academic year to identify factors associated with student math achievement. Using Python, Power BI, correlation analysis, and Linear Regression modeling, the analysis explores how attendance, chronic absenteeism, economic disadvantage, and other district characteristics relate to academic performance.

The project combines exploratory data analysis, statistical modeling, and interactive dashboard development to uncover meaningful patterns in educational outcomes and support data-informed decision making.

## Data Scope

* Geographic Scope: Texas School Districts
* Academic Year: 2024–2025
* Unit of Analysis: School District
* Outcome Variable: STAAR Math Meets Grade Level (%)

## Research Questions

1. Which district-level factors are most strongly associated with math achievement?
2. How do chronic absenteeism and economic disadvantage relate to district performance?
3. Can district math achievement be estimated using attendance and demographic indicators?

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Scikit-Learn
* Power BI

## Key Findings

* Economic disadvantage (-0.582) and chronic absenteeism (-0.576) exhibited the strongest negative relationships with math achievement.
* Districts with higher levels of absenteeism and economic disadvantage generally demonstrated lower math achievement outcomes.
* A Linear Regression model using attendance rate, chronic absenteeism rate, and economic disadvantage percentage explained approximately 48% of the variation in district math achievement (R² = 0.483).
* Several districts with high levels of economic disadvantage achieved above-average math performance, suggesting that additional factors contribute to district success.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard allows users to explore district performance, achievement metrics, and relationships between academic outcomes and district characteristics through interactive visualizations and filtering.

## Future Enhancements

Potential future work includes expanding the analysis to multiple academic years, incorporating longitudinal district trends, and evaluating more advanced machine learning models for achievement forecasting.
